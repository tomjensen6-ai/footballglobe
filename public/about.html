About the data

What this map shows, where the information comes from, and what it does not guarantee.

Coverage

Coverage varies by country and division, and some leagues may be incomplete. A country or competition being absent does not mean it has no football — only that it is not currently mapped here.

Stadiums are included only when sufficiently reliable location data is available. If a venue cannot be resolved with reasonable confidence, it is left out. This means the map may under-report coverage rather than knowingly display a ground in the wrong location.

Shared stadiums

Where several clubs share a stadium, they are shown together on one pin rather than as separate entries. A single pin may therefore represent more than one team.

Accuracy

Stadium, club, capacity and competition information may be incomplete, outdated or incorrect. Venue details change: stadiums are renamed, clubs move and capacities are revised. The stadium dataset is updated periodically rather than continuously.

Always confirm fixtures, venue details and travel arrangements with official sources before making a booking.

Independence

VeylorCraft Away is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any club, league, federation or venue.

Club names, stadium names and related trademarks belong to their respective owners and are used for identification purposes only.

Sources and credits

The interactive map is provided by Google Maps, with Google's required attribution displayed on the map.

Stadium, club and competition information is compiled from third-party sports data sources, including API-Football.

Weather forecasts are not provided by this site. Where a forecast link is shown, it opens Windy in a new tab.

Corrections

Corrections are welcome. If a stadium is missing, incorrectly described or shown in the wrong location, please get in touch and include the name of the venue and the corrected information.
