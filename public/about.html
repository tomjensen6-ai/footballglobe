About the data

What this map shows, where it comes from, and what it does not guarantee.

Coverage

Coverage varies by country and division, and some leagues may be incomplete. A country or competition being absent does not mean it has no football — only that it is not currently mapped here.

Stadiums are included only when sufficiently reliable location data is available. Where a venue cannot be resolved to a location we are confident in, it is left out rather than placed approximately. This means the map under-reports rather than guesses, and some grounds you expect to find will be missing.

Shared stadiums

Where several clubs share a stadium, they are shown together on one pin rather than as separate entries. A single pin may therefore represent more than one team.

Accuracy

Stadium, club, capacity, weather and other information may be incomplete, outdated or incorrect. Venue details change — grounds are renamed, clubs move, capacities are revised — and this map is generated periodically rather than updated live.

Always confirm fixtures, venue details and travel arrangements with official sources before making a booking.

Independence

VeylorCraft Away is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any club, league, federation or venue. Club names, stadium names and related marks belong to their respective owners and are used here for identification only.

Sources and credits

Mapping is provided by Google Maps, with attribution shown on the map itself. Stadium, club and competition data comes from third-party sports data providers. Weather information is supplied by a third-party forecast service.

Detailed attribution for each data source is being finalised and will be published here.

Contact

Corrections are welcome — if a venue is wrong, missing or misplaced, it is useful to know.
